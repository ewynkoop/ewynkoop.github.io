01About

I grew up in a small, rural town just outside Charlottesville, Virginia, and now I study Applied Math at Harvard, with a focus in Engineering and a secondary in Physics.

I went to a public high school, and in my sophomore year I was accepted into a half-day STEM program, where I earned an associate-degree level of college STEM credit. I'm passionate about all things STEM. I love learning and solving problems.

This past summer I worked on the Product Team at CollageAI, an EdTech startup that builds active learning environments for students. The platform has been piloted at institutions including Harvard, Cornell, and Duke.

I haven't mapped out the next 30 years of my life, but I'm open to careers in finance, product management, consulting, engineering, venture capital, entrepreneurship, and more.
